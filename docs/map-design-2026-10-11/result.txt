Limits

- Nearby markers can overlap at small zoom; place list and zoom provide access.
- CCTV not connected in this change. Verified national installation dataset is available as CSV and can be ingested server-side and exposed via radius-query API.
- Seoul Ansimi CCTV public dataset is discontinued. Installation data does not verify live operation or provide live surveillance video.
